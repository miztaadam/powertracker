- Reuse kit and approved poses where they fit (Squat and its variants from the main lifts for depth and bar position, Front squat's rack position, Leg press and its sled, the Smith machine, the plyo/squat box, the deficit platform as a step, kettlebell and dumbbell props). - Kit pieces you will likely need (manufacturer-neutral typical size with units in a comment, each with a KitTest case): hack squat machine (angled sled with back pad and shoulder pads, foot plate), lever (pendulum/plate-loaded) squat machine, leg extension machine (seat, back pad, shin roller, pivot at the knee), step/box for step-ups (knee-height or the guide's height), push sled (uprights and skids), and whatever else the list needs.
- Laziness rule (Figures.kt comment): an area's map must not build its figures (map of `Lazy<ExerciseFigure>`); AreaMapsLazyTest must keep passing.
- Pins/spotter only where the guide's safety line asks (constraints.md).
- Camera: squats, forward/reverse lunges, split squats, step-ups, leg extensions and the sled push read from the side. Lateral lunge, curtsy lunge and lateral step-up move across the camera — front view (lesson 9); say why for each. One-leg versions (single-leg box squat, single-leg leg extension, single-leg leg press) must differ plainly from the two-leg version at thumbnail (lesson 10): the free leg clearly apart from the working leg. Walking lunge vs lunge: the guide's difference is stepping through — choose two poses that show travel (e.g. the lunge bottom, then the next step's drive) if the kit allows; otherwise captions the picture shows. Jump squat: the END is in the air (feet clearly off the floor, legs straight) — a figure with no floor contact is new; make the checks allow it for that pose only.
- Key checks per entry: squats → depth the guide gives (thighs level / below), where the load is (goblet at the chest, dumbbells at the sides, Zercher in the elbow crooks, kettlebells racked, bar on the back or front), feet flat, knees over the feet, torso angle sensible for the load position (BarOverMidfoot where a bar is used); machine squats → back/shoulders on the pads, feet on the plate, depth; lunges/split squats → front shin near vertical (or the guide's), front thigh level at the bottom, back knee just off the floor (bound its height), torso upright, step length sensible (bound both knees' angles — lesson 5: no crouch or kneel by accident); step-ups → whole foot on the box, finish standing tall on the box, trailing leg per guide; leg extensions → back on pad, thighs on seat, shin from hanging (about 90° knee) to nearly straight, roller on the lower shin; sled push → body line leaning into the sled at the guide's angle, arms straight or bent per guide, one leg driving. Each entry gets a focused test in a new `BatchQuadsTest.kt` with real bounds.
