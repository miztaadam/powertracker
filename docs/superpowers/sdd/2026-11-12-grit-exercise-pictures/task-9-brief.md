- Pins/spotter only where the guide's safety line asks (constraints.md).
- Camera: curls and triceps work are elbow flexion/extension in the sagittal plane — side view for nearly all. Things that must show: the upper arm's position (at the side for standing curls and pushdowns; behind the body on an incline curl; in front on the pad for preacher/spider/concentration; beside the head for overhead extensions; in line with the torso for the kickback; vertical for skull crushers) and that it stays put between the panels while the forearm moves. Grip differences: a hammer (neutral) grip shows the dumbbell in profile, a supinated curl shows it end-on (lesson 2) — so DB curl vs Hammer curl must differ by that; reverse curl vs curl cannot differ from the side except by caption (choose captions the picture shows, lesson 7). Wrist curls: only the hand moves — forearm supported on the thigh/bench, the hand bending down then up; check the kit can draw a bent wrist both ways and that the difference shows at thumbnail (if not, say so). Triangle vs close-grip push-up: hand spacing is across the camera — consider the head-on front view (lesson 1) if it shows the hands' triangle better, or keep side view and choose captions it shows; your call with a comment.
- Key checks per entry: curls → upper arm within a few degrees of its guide position in both panels (elbow does not drift), forearm from near-straight to the guide's top (do not over-curl past the guide), wrists straight, torso upright (or the guide's lean), contacts for seated/pad versions; pushdowns → elbows at the sides in both panels, forearms from about level to straight down, torso lean per guide; extensions → upper arm fixed (vertical / beside the head / the guide's slight tilt back for skull crushers), elbows from the guide's bend to straight, contacts (head/back on bench); kickback → upper arm level with the torso, forearm from hanging to straight; dip → upright torso (contrast with the Chest dip's lean), elbows back; push-ups → as Push-up plus hand position. Legs bounded where the guide implies straight/seated (lesson 5). Each entry gets a focused test in a new `BatchArmsTest.kt` with real bounds.
