- Lessons from the arm and quad batches: (11) a free or far arm must be given a real pose — `HANG` on a leaning torso lies along the torso and can pass through the hip or the bench (one arm figure did); bound the far wrist in the test; (12) a foot on the ball of the foot (the back foot of a split stance, a calf raise's top, a sled push's drive foot) uses the raised-heel foot the quad batch's fix round added, so the heel is clearly up and the shin slopes — a shin lying along the floor reads as kneeling; (13) a pose in the air needs a plain gap under the feet (35–40 cm for the jump squat) or it reads as standing; (14) START and END must differ by what the guide's step says: if the guide says stand up / straighten / lock out, the START or END must show it — a stance or set-up that stops the joint reaching it is the thing to change, not the bound.

- Each pose from that exercise's own guide (setup + steps); captions ≤24 characters from the guide's wording. Positions the guide doesn't fix: sourced from the allowed domains only (WebSearch with allowed_domains), cited in a code comment (URL + what it supports), conservative where sources disagree.
- Reuse kit and approved poses where they fit (RDL and Deadlift hinge poses, the back-extension bench for the glute-ham raise if it fits, the flat bench for hip thrusts, the cable station with an ankle strap, kettlebell, the lying/side-lying floor poses from Side-lying external rotation, the leg extension machine's seat for the seated leg curl). - Kit pieces you will likely need (manufacturer-neutral typical size with units in a comment, each with a KitTest case): lying, seated and standing leg curl machines (pad on the lower calf/ankle, pivot at the knee), glute-ham bench (GHD) with foot plate and rollers, reverse hyper bench, hip thrust machine (belt/pad over the hips), hip abduction/adduction machine (seat with knee pads), stability ball (65 cm typical), mini band (around the knees or ankles), ankle strap for cables, and whatever else the list needs.
- Laziness rule (Figures.kt comment): an area's map must not build its figures (map of `Lazy<ExerciseFigure>`); AreaMapsLazyTest must keep passing.
- Pins/spotter only where the guide's safety line asks (constraints.md).
- Camera: hinges, leg curls, bridges, thrusts, the reverse hyper, the quadruped hip extension, the Nordic curl and the glute-ham raise read from the side. The abduction/adduction group moves across the camera: machine hip abduction and adduction (knees apart / together) and the lateral band walk are front views (lesson 9); cable hip abduction, side-lying hip abduction and the clamshell — pick the view where the leg's lift shows (a side-lying lifter seen from the front of the body shows the top leg rising; a standing cable abduction seen from the front shows the leg swinging out). Say why for each. One-leg versions (single-leg RDL, single-leg glute bridge, single-arm swing) must differ plainly from the two-sided version at thumbnail (lesson 10).
- Key checks per entry: hinges → back flat (torso angle the guide gives at the bottom), knees soft or straight per guide (stiff-leg vs RDL must differ visibly: knee bend and how far the bar goes down), bar/dumbbells close to the legs, hips back; swings → the hinge at the bottom with the bell behind the knees/between the legs, the top standing tall with the bell at the guide's height (chest/shoulder, not overhead unless the guide says), arms straight; leg curls → thighs/hips on the pad, the pad on the lower leg, knee from straight to the guide's bend, hips not lifting; Nordic/GHR → knees on the pad, ankles held, body straight from knee to head, the lowered position per guide; bridges/thrusts → shoulders on the bench (thrust) or floor (bridge), shins near vertical at the top, hips in line with shoulders and knees at the top (no over-arch), bar/dumbbell over the hip crease; reverse hyper → hips at the bench edge, legs from hanging to in line with the torso; abduction/adduction → the moving leg's angle from the midline at both ends per guide, pelvis level, torso upright or lying straight. Legs bounded everywhere (lesson 5). Each entry gets a focused test in a new `BatchHamstringsGlutesTest.kt` with real bounds.
