## What to build

After this task every catalogue exercise has a figure: 225.

- Reuse kit and approved poses where they fit (Leg press and its sled for the leg press calf raise, the Smith machine, the standing poses, the deficit platform/step as a calf block, the push-up plank line for the front plank, the side-lying pose, the quadruped pose from the quadruped hip extension, the cable stations, the stability ball, the back-extension and crunch-like machine seats). - Kit pieces you will likely need (manufacturer-neutral typical size with units in a comment, each with a KitTest case): calf block/step (about 10 cm), standing calf raise machine (shoulder pads), seated calf raise machine (knee pad), ab wheel, crunch machine, farmer's-walk handles or heavy dumbbells, a weight plate held by pinch, and whatever else the list needs.
- Laziness rule (Figures.kt comment): an area's map must not build its figures (map of `Lazy<ExerciseFigure>`); AreaMapsLazyTest must keep passing.
- Pins/spotter only where the guide's safety line asks (constraints.md).
- Camera: calf raises read from the side; the whole movement is the ankle — heels dropped below the block's edge, then up on the toes. The rise is small: draw the heel drop and the rise at the guide's full range and check both show at thumbnail size; if the kit's ankle limits stop the range showing, say so. Crunches, planks, dead bug, bird dog, rollouts and the carries read from the side; the side plank, Pallof press and cable woodchop are judged case by case: the side plank reads from the front of the body (the body's line from feet to head with the hips up), the Pallof press and the woodchop are about resisting or making a turn across the body — pick the view that shows the cable's line and the arms' travel (front for Pallof: arms pressing straight out toward the camera would be foreshortened, so a view from above is not available — a side-on view with the stack beside the lifter may show the press out and back best; say why). Carries: START and END would be the same pose — show the two things the guide stresses instead (e.g. the pick-up or standing tall, then mid-stride with the load at the guide's position), and say in the report what each panel shows. Suitcase carry (one side loaded) and farmer's walk (both sides) differ across the camera: front view for at least the suitcase carry so the one-sided load and upright torso show (lessons 9 and 10). Waiter's carry: load overhead on one straight arm.
- Key checks per entry: calf raises → balls of the feet on the block, heels below it at START and above at END by the guide's range (bound the ankle angle both ways), knees straight (or bent 90° seated, hips bent for the donkey raise), pads/bar where the guide says; crunches → lower back on the floor, shoulder blades off at the top, hips not flexing (crunch) or hips rolling up (reverse crunch), the bicycle's opposite elbow and knee; cable/machine crunch → hips fixed, spine curling, hands by the head; planks → straight line from heels to head within a few degrees, elbows under shoulders, hips neither sagging nor piked; dead bug / bird dog → opposite arm and leg extended, lower back flat / hips level; rollouts → arms nearly straight, body line long at the reach, no hip sag, return to knees-under-hips; Pallof → arms from chest to straight in front, torso square to the front (no turn); woodchop → the diagonal from the guide's start to its end, arms nearly straight; carries → torso upright (no lean toward or away from the load, bound it), shoulders level, load at the guide's position. Legs bounded everywhere (lesson 5). Each entry gets a focused test in new `BatchCalvesTest.kt` and `BatchCoreTest.kt` with real bounds.
