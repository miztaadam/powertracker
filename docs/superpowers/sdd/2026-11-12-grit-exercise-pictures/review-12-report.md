Fix: assert the arms' depth, for example that each arm's `out` has the sign that points toward the camera (as the floor crunch's `nearArm.out 30..90` does, which my same-style flip of its hint did fail) or that the elbow point lies in front of the shoulder-to-hand line in 3D.

If the skeleton cannot do that without a torso turn, keep what is drawn but change START's caption to something the panel shows ("Handle overhead") and tighten the arm bound to 0..25 so it does not drift further from "arms straight".
