## Decisions

- **No fix without a symptom.** "Buggy" is not one. The Mac session's job is evidence: what does real iOS Safari do?
- **No rollback below 1.17; fix forward** (`CLAUDE.md`).
- **Polish stays out of any fix for this,** and nothing in `Dialog`, `Menu`, `historyCloses.ts` or the router is
  touched for polish while this is open.
- **The suspect, from reading only:** 1.17.4's iPhone Back path (`web/src/ui/components/historyCloses.ts`). After a
  sheet or menu is closed by a tap it calls `history.go(-1)` and holds the app's own history calls (a tab tap among
  them) until that step's `popstate` arrives; if Safari is late or silent, up to 4.5 s (`POP_CHECK_MS` 1500 ×
  `UNMOVED_CHECKS` 3). Never observed. It may be nothing.
- **The other candidate:** the report predates 1.17.5 (a finished session came back as open after a reload), or is
  about open copies reloading for the three updates since 2026-11-22. Ask which version the reporter has.
